Effortlessly connect your WordPress site to Kubota Connect and retrieve dealer-specific products, finance offers, and promotion slides. Enhance your website's functionality by showcasing the latest Kubota equipment, offers, and promotions tailored specifically to your dealership.

### Features:

*   **Seamless Integration**: Connect to Kubota Connect with ease, allowing your website to pull in the most current data.
*   **Dealer-Specific Products**: Automatically display products specific to your dealership, ensuring your customers see the right inventory.
*   **Finance Offers**: Keep your site updated with the latest finance offers from Kubota, providing valuable information to potential buyers.
*   **Promotion Slides**: Showcase current promotions with attractive slides, enhancing your site's visual appeal and marketing efforts.
*   **Kubota Corporate Design**: Easily add Kubota's design for products, offers, and slides are displayed on your site or create a template to match your brand’s look and feel.
*   **Regular Updates**: Ensure your site always features the latest Kubota offerings with automatic updates from Kubota Connect.

### Installation:

1.  Download the Kubota Connect plugin from <https://api.kubota.io/dealer-api-docs>.
1.  In your WordPress dashboard, choose **Plugins > Add new**.
1.  Click **Upload Plugin** at the top of the page.
1.  Click **Choose File**, locate the plugin .zip file, then click **Install Now**.
1.  After the installation is complete, click **Activate Plugin**.
1.  Navigate to the plugin settings page to configure your Kubota Connect API key token.
1.  Save changes and start showcasing dealer-specific products, finance offers, and promotion slides on your site.
